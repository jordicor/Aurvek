## Short answer

Click the assistant's photo or initial in the chat header to open **Assistant details**, with its picture, name, description, and available extension levels. The panel matches your current theme.

## Steps

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click the assistant's photo or initial beside the conversation title. The button is labelled **View assistant details** and also works with the keyboard.
3. Click the photo in the panel to open the full-resolution image in the image viewer. Press **Escape** or click outside the image to close it.
4. Close the details panel with **Close**, **Escape**, or a click outside it to return to the same position in your conversation.

## Notes

- The assistant card also appears at the very beginning of a conversation.
- Long conversations load recent messages first. Scroll up to load older messages; the card appears above the first message once you reach the beginning of the full history.
- Opening the details panel does not load older messages or change your scroll position.
