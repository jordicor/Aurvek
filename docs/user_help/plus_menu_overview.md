## Short answer

The plus (+) menu is at the bottom-left of the chat input. It provides quick access to attachments, audio recording, reasoning, web search, browser voice, telephone calls, and Multi-AI comparison. Available items depend on the conversation, model, prompt, and account.

## Steps

1. Click the **+** button next to the message input to open the menu.
2. The menu contains the following options:
   - **Attach files** -- Adds supported images, PDFs, or text/code files to your message.
   - **Record audio** -- Starts a voice recording using your microphone. The recording is transcribed to text and inserted into the message input.
   - **Reasoning** -- Selects one of the reasoning modes supported by the current model. The available choices and any custom token range vary by model.
   - **Web search** -- Toggles real-time web search on or off. When active, the AI can search the internet for current information. Some prompts may force this on or hide it entirely.
   - **AI Voice** -- Starts a real-time voice session through your browser microphone.
   - **Call me** -- Assigns this conversation to telephone calls and lets you call your verified profile number now or once at a scheduled time.
   - **Multi-AI Compare** -- Sends your message to multiple AI models simultaneously and shows their responses side by side. Only visible when the feature is available for the current conversation.
3. Click outside the menu or select an option to close it.

## Notes

- Not all options are visible at all times. Availability depends on the current model, prompt configuration, and your account permissions.
- **AI Voice** and **Call me** are different: one uses the browser, while the other uses the telephone network.
- The plus button shows a small dot when a configurable feature such as reasoning, web search, or Multi-AI is active.
