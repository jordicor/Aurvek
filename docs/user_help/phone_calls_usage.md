## Short answer

Aurvek can call your verified profile phone now or at a scheduled time. Where incoming calls are enabled, you can call Aurvek from that phone and continue the assigned conversation. **Call me** uses the telephone network; **AI Voice** uses your browser microphone.

## Steps

1. In **Settings > Profile**, verify your phone number. A saved **Time Zone** helps the assistant interpret call-time requests.
2. Open the conversation you want to use and select **+ > Call me**.
3. Select **Use this conversation for phone calls**. **Call now** and **Schedule for later** also assign it automatically.
4. Choose an immediate call or a date and time. This picker uses the browser's current time zone. Aurvek only calls your saved profile number.
5. If your account provides an incoming-call number, call it from your verified profile number. The assigned conversation supplies its prompt and history.
6. If the prompt permits it, ask the assistant naturally to call now or schedule a time. Without a saved time zone, it may ask for your city and country.
7. Use **Settings > Calls** to see call history or cancel the pending scheduled call. During a live call, you or the assistant can end it.

## Notes

- Only one conversation is assigned to calls per account. Assigning another replaces it.
- Telephone assignment is independent of WhatsApp and Telegram, so the same conversation may use telephone plus one messaging channel.
- Only one future call can be scheduled at a time. Calls are one-time, not recurring.
- The call transcript is added to the conversation. Raw call audio is not retained by default; a prompt may explicitly enable retention.
- Service availability and sufficient balance are required. Locked or incognito conversations cannot start or schedule calls.
